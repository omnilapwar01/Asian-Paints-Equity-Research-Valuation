# Asian Paints Ltd. – Equity Research & Valuation

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive equity research and financial analysis of **Asian Paints Limited**, one of India's leading paint and home décor companies. It combines company research, historical financial performance analysis, working capital assessment, peer comparison, and Excel-based financial valuation modelling.

The project aims to evaluate the company's financial position, operating performance, valuation drivers, and potential investment considerations using publicly available financial information and structured financial modelling techniques.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze the company's business model, industry position, and major business segments.
- Evaluate historical financial performance, including revenue, EBIT, profit after tax, and total assets.
- Assess working capital efficiency through receivable days, inventory days, payable days, and the cash conversion cycle.
- Compare valuation multiples and financial metrics with selected industry peers.
- Develop an integrated financial model using historical financial statements and forecast assumptions.
- Estimate the company's cost of capital and assess valuation under different scenarios.
